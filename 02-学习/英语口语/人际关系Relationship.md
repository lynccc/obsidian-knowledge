# 🗣️ 人际关系 Relationship

## 场景简介
> 描述友情、恋爱、分手、相处等人际关系的英语表达。这些词汇在日常聊天和社交场合中非常高频。

## 核心词汇

### 1. To break up
- **英文**：To break up
- **中文**：分手
- **例句**：They broke up after three years together.（他们在一起三年后分手了。）
- **💡 用法提示**：break up 也可指朋友关系破裂。

### 2. To drift apart
- **英文**：To drift apart
- **中文**：渐行渐远
- **例句**：We drifted apart after college.（大学毕业后我们渐行渐远。）

### 3. To enjoy someone's company
- **英文**：To enjoy someone's company
- **中文**：喜欢和某人在一起
- **例句**：I really enjoy your company.（我真的很喜欢和你在一起。）

### 4. To fall for
- **英文**：To fall for
- **中文**：爱上/迷恋
- **例句**：She fell for him the moment they met.（她一见到他就爱上了。）

### 5. To fall head over heels in love
- **英文**：To fall head over heels in love
- **中文**：神魂颠倒地爱上
- **例句**：He fell head over heels in love with her.（他神魂颠倒地爱上了她。）

### 6. To fall out with
- **英文**：To fall out with
- **中文**：闹翻/吵架后不再来往
- **例句**：I fell out with my best friend over money.（我和最好的朋友因为钱闹翻了。）

### 7. To get on like a house on fire
- **英文**：To get on like a house on fire
- **中文**：一见如故/相处得非常好
- **例句**：They got on like a house on fire from day one.（他们从第一天起就相处得非常好。）

### 8. To get on well with
- **英文**：To get on well with
- **中文**：和……相处融洽
- **例句**：I get on well with my colleagues.（我和同事相处融洽。）

### 9. To go back years
- **英文**：To go back years
- **中文**：认识很多年了
- **例句**：We go back years — we've been friends since childhood.（我们认识很多年了——从小就是朋友。）

### 10. To have a lot in common
- **英文**：To have a lot in common
- **中文**：有很多共同点
- **例句**：We have a lot in common — we both love hiking.（我们有很多共同点——都喜欢徒步。）

### 11. To have ups and downs
- **英文**：To have ups and downs
- **中文**：有起起落落/经历波折
- **例句**：Every relationship has its ups and downs.（每段关系都有起起落落。）

### 12. To hit it off
- **英文**：To hit it off
- **中文**：一拍即合
- **例句**：We hit it off immediately at the party.（我们在派对上一拍即合。）

### 13. To be in a relationship
- **英文**：To be in a relationship
- **中文**：在恋爱中
- **例句**：Are you in a relationship?（你在谈恋爱吗？）

### 14. To keep in touch with
- **英文**：To keep in touch with
- **中文**：保持联系
- **例句**：Let's keep in touch after graduation.（毕业后我们保持联系。）

### 15. To lose touch with
- **英文**：To lose touch with
- **中文**：失去联系
- **例句**：I've lost touch with most of my school friends.（我和大多数校友都失去了联系。）

### 16. Love at first sight
- **英文**：Love at first sight
- **中文**：一见钟情
- **例句**：It was love at first sight when they met.（他们相遇时一见钟情。）

### 17. To pop the question
- **英文**：To pop the question
- **中文**：求婚
- **例句**：He popped the question on Valentine's Day.（他在情人节求婚了。）

### 18. To see eye to eye
- **英文**：To see eye to eye
- **中文**：意见一致
- **例句**：We don't always see eye to eye, but we respect each other.（我们不总是意见一致，但互相尊重。）

### 19. To settle down
- **英文**：To settle down
- **中文**：安定下来（结婚成家）
- **例句**：They decided to settle down and start a family.（他们决定安定下来组建家庭。）

### 20. To tie the knot
- **英文**：To tie the knot
- **中文**：结婚
- **例句**：They tied the knot last summer.（他们去年夏天结婚了。）

### 21. To be well matched
- **英文**：To be well matched
- **中文**：很般配
- **例句**：They're well matched — both love traveling.（他们很般配——都爱旅行。）

## 常用句型
- How did you two meet?（你们怎么认识的？）
- We've been friends for years.（我们是多年的朋友了。）
- We have a lot in common.（我们有很多共同点。）
- They're very well matched.（他们很般配。）
- We keep in touch through social media.（我们通过社交媒体保持联系。）

## 📝 学习建议
- 这些表达非常适合描述人际关系，多用在日常对话中
- 注意习语的理解：like a house on fire、head over heels 等要整体记忆
- 用这些词汇描述你自己的人际关系，加深印象
