# 🗣️ 购物 Shopping

## 场景简介
> 逛商场、网上购物、讨价还价、退换商品等场景中常用的英语表达。无论是实体店还是网购，这些词汇都能帮你轻松购物。

## 核心词汇

### 1. To be careful with money
- **英文**：To be careful with money
- **中文**：花钱谨慎
- **例句**：You should be careful with money and not overspend.（你应该花钱谨慎，不要超支。）

### 2. Carrier bag
- **英文**：Carrier bag
- **中文**：购物袋
- **例句**：Do you need a carrier bag?（你需要购物袋吗？）

### 3. Customer service
- **英文**：Customer service
- **中文**：客户服务
- **例句**：The customer service in this store is excellent.（这家店的客户服务非常好。）

### 4. To get into debt
- **英文**：To get into debt
- **中文**：负债
- **例句**：Don't get into debt by buying things you can't afford.（不要因为买不起的东西而负债。）

### 5. To give someone the hard sell
- **英文**：To give someone the hard sell
- **中文**：向某人强行推销
- **例句**：The salesman gave me the hard sell, so I ended up buying it.（销售员强行推销，结果我买了。）

### 6. Loyalty card
- **英文**：Loyalty card
- **中文**：会员卡/积分卡
- **例句**：Do you have a loyalty card? You can earn points.（你有会员卡吗？可以积分。）

### 7. Must-have product
- **英文**：Must-have product
- **中文**：必买产品
- **例句**：This phone is the must-have product of the year.（这款手机是今年的必买产品。）

### 8. To be on a tight budget
- **英文**：To be on a tight budget
- **中文**：预算紧张
- **例句**：I'm on a tight budget, so I can't afford expensive brands.（我预算紧张，买不起大牌。）

### 9. To be on commission
- **英文**：To be on commission
- **中文**：按提成拿工资
- **例句**：The shop assistants are on commission, so they're very pushy.（店员是拿提成的，所以很积极推销。）

### 10. To pay in cash
- **英文**：To pay in cash
- **中文**：用现金支付
- **例句**：Can I pay in cash?（我能用现金支付吗？）

### 11. To pick up a bargain
- **英文**：To pick up a bargain
- **中文**：买到便宜货
- **例句**：I picked up a bargain at the flea market.（我在跳蚤市场买到了便宜货。）

### 12. To run up a credit card bill
- **英文**：To run up a credit card bill
- **中文**：刷爆信用卡
- **例句**：She ran up a huge credit card bill during the sales.（她在打折期间刷爆了信用卡。）

### 13. To shop around
- **英文**：To shop around
- **中文**：货比三家
- **例句**：It's worth shopping around for the best deal.（值得货比三家找最好的价格。）

### 14. To shop until you drop
- **英文**：To shop until you drop
- **中文**：逛到腿软/疯狂购物
- **例句**：We shopped until we dropped at the outlet mall.（我们在奥特莱斯逛到腿软。）

### 15. To slash prices
- **英文**：To slash prices
- **中文**：大幅降价
- **例句**：The store slashed prices by 50% for the clearance sale.（清仓大甩卖，全场五折。）

### 16. To snap up a bargain
- **英文**：To snap up a bargain
- **中文**：抢购便宜货
- **例句**：Shoppers snapped up bargains on Black Friday.（购物者在黑色星期五抢购便宜货。）

### 17. To try something on
- **英文**：To try something on
- **中文**：试穿
- **例句**：Can I try this dress on?（我能试穿这条裙子吗？）

### 18. To be value for money
- **英文**：To be value for money
- **中文**：物有所值
- **例句**：This restaurant is really value for money.（这家餐厅真的很物有所值。）

### 19. Window shopping
- **英文**：Window shopping
- **中文**：逛街看橱窗（只看不买）
- **例句**：We went window shopping but didn't buy anything.（我们去逛街看了看但什么都没买。）

## 常用句型
- Is there a sale on at the moment?（现在有打折吗？）
- Can I try this on?（我能试穿一下吗？）
- I'm just looking, thanks.（我只是看看，谢谢。）
- Do you have this in a different size?（这个有别的尺码吗？）
- Can I pay by card?（我能刷卡吗？）
- This is a real bargain!（这真是便宜货！）

## 📝 学习建议
- 记住购物场景的完整对话流程：进店→询问→试穿→付款
- 学会用 budget、bargain、sale 等词描述省钱购物
- 看英文购物 Vlog 或 unboxing 视频，学习真实购物用语
