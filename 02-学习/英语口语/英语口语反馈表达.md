# 🗣️ 英语口语反馈表达

## 场景简介
> 在对话中给予反馈是非常重要的沟通技能。无论是表扬、鼓励还是委婉地指出问题，这些表达让你的回应更自然、更有礼貌。

## 一、正面反馈 Giving Positive Feedback

### 1. I like it!
- **英文**：I like it!
- **中文**：我喜欢！
- **例句**：I like it! The colour is perfect. 我喜欢！颜色很完美。

### 2. I love it!
- **英文**：I love it!
- **中文**：我太喜欢了！
- **例句**：I love it! This is exactly what I wanted. 我太喜欢了！这正是我想要的。

### 3. Good job!
- **英文**：Good job!
- **中文**：干得好！
- **例句**：Good job on the presentation! 演讲做得很棒！

### 4. That's correct!
- **英文**：That's correct!
- **中文**：没错！
- **例句**：That's correct! You got the right answer. 没错！你答对了。

### 5. Well done!
- **英文**：Well done!
- **中文**：做得好！
- **例句**：Well done! You passed the exam. 做得好！你通过了考试。

### 6. That's nice!
- **英文**：That's nice!
- **中文**：真不错！
- **例句**：That's nice! I really like your new dress. 真不错！我真的很喜欢你的新裙子。

## 二、高度赞扬 Giving Very Positive Feedback

### 7. Awesome!
- **英文**：Awesome!
- **中文**：太棒了！
- **例句**：Awesome! That was an amazing performance! 太棒了！那是一场精彩的表演！

### 8. Brilliant!
- **英文**：Brilliant!
- **中文**：太出色了！
- **例句**：Brilliant idea! Let's do it. 出色的想法！就这么做。

### 9. Excellent!
- **英文**：Excellent!
- **中文**：太优秀了！
- **例句**：Excellent work! I'm very impressed. 优秀的工作！我印象深刻。

### 10. Fantastic!
- **英文**：Fantastic!
- **中文**：太棒了！
- **例句**：Fantastic! You did it perfectly. 太棒了！你做得完美。

### 11. Magnificent!
- **英文**：Magnificent!
- **中文**：太壮观了！
- **例句**：The view from the top was magnificent! 山顶的景色太壮观了！

### 12. Outstanding!
- **英文**：Outstanding!
- **中文**：杰出的！
- **例句**：Your performance was outstanding! 你的表现很杰出！

### 13. Terrific!
- **英文**：Terrific!
- **中文**：太好了！
- **例句**：Terrific job on the project! 项目做得太好了！

## 三、鼓励性反馈 Giving Encouraging Feedback

### 14. I'm impressed!
- **英文**：I'm impressed!
- **中文**：我很佩服！
- **例句**：I'm impressed by how quickly you learned. 你学得这么快，我很佩服。

### 15. That's more like it.
- **英文**：That's more like it.
- **中文**：这才对嘛。
- **例句**：That's more like it! You're doing much better now. 这才对嘛！你现在做得好多了。

### 16. That's much better.
- **英文**：That's much better.
- **中文**：好多了。
- **例句**：That's much better than last time. 比上次好多了。

### 17. You are doing well.
- **英文**：You are doing well.
- **中文**：你做得很好。
- **例句**：You are doing well — keep practising! 你做得很好——继续练习！

### 18. You're getting there.
- **英文**：You're getting there.
- **中文**：你快成功了。
- **例句**：You're getting there! Just a bit more practice. 你快成功了！再练一下。

### 19. You've improved a lot.
- **英文**：You've improved a lot.
- **中文**：你进步很大。
- **例句**：Your English has improved a lot! 你的英语进步很大！

### 20. Keep up the good work!
- **英文**：Keep up the good work!
- **中文**：继续保持！
- **例句**：Keep up the good work and you'll succeed. 继续保持，你会成功的。

### 21. You're on the right track now.
- **英文**：You're on the right track now.
- **中文**：你现在方向对了。
- **例句**：You're on the right track now — keep going! 你现在方向对了——继续！

### 22. You're getting better every day.
- **英文**：You're getting better every day.
- **中文**：你每天都在进步。
- **例句**：You're getting better every day. I can see the improvement. 你每天都在进步。我能看到你的进步。

## 四、委婉否定反馈 Giving Negative Feedback

### 23. Not exactly.
- **英文**：Not exactly.
- **中文**：不完全对。
- **例句**：Not exactly. Try again with a different approach. 不完全对。换个方法再试试。

### 24. Good try but...
- **英文**：Good try but...
- **中文**：不错的尝试，但是……
- **例句**：Good try, but that's not quite right. 不错的尝试，但不太对。

### 25. That is almost it.
- **英文**：That is almost it.
- **中文**：差不多了。
- **例句**：That is almost it! Just one small mistake. 差不多了！就一个小错误。

### 26. Unfortunately, not.
- **英文**：Unfortunately, not.
- **中文**：很遗憾，不对。
- **例句**：Unfortunately, not. Let me explain the correct answer. 很遗憾，不对。让我解释正确答案。

### 27. That's not quite correct.
- **英文**：That's not quite correct.
- **中文**：不完全正确。
- **例句**：That's not quite correct, but you're close. 不完全正确，但你很接近了。

### 28. I'm afraid that's not quite right.
- **英文**：I'm afraid that's not quite right.
- **中文**：恐怕不太对。
- **例句**：I'm afraid that's not quite right. Let me help you. 恐怕不太对。让我帮你。

## 五、继续尝试反馈 Keep Trying Feedback

### 29. Have another try.
- **英文**：Have another try.
- **中文**：再试一次。
- **例句**：Have another try — I'm sure you'll get it this time. 再试一次——我相信你这次能行。

### 30. Give it another shot.
- **英文**：Give it another shot.
- **中文**：再试一次。
- **例句**：Give it another shot! You're so close. 再试一次！你很接近了。

### 31. There's no hurry.
- **英文**：There's no hurry.
- **中文**：不用着急。
- **例句**：There's no hurry. Take your time. 不用着急，慢慢来。

### 32. We have plenty of time.
- **英文**：We have plenty of time.
- **中文**：我们有很多时间。
- **例句**：We have plenty of time, so don't worry. 我们有很多时间，别担心。

## 常用句型
- Great job! I'm really impressed. 做得好！我真的很佩服。
- That's not quite right, but you're almost there. 不太对，但你快成功了。
- Keep up the good work! 继续保持！
- Have another try — you'll get it eventually. 再试一次——你最终会成功的。
- I'm afraid that's not quite correct. Let me explain. 恐怕不太对。让我解释一下。
- You've improved a lot since last time. 你比上次进步了很多。
- That's more like it! You're on the right track. 这才对嘛！你方向对了。
- Don't worry, there's no hurry. 别担心，不着急。

## 📝 学习建议
- "Good job" 和 "Well done" 是最常用的表扬，日常必备
- 委婉否定时用 "not exactly" 或 "I'm afraid that's not quite right"，比直接说 "no" 更礼貌
- "You're getting there" 是鼓励别人继续努力的好表达
- "Give it another shot" 比 "try again" 更口语化、更亲切
- 反馈表达的层次：正面 → 高度赞扬 → 鼓励 → 委婉否定 → 继续尝试
