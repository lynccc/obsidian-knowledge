# 职业道德与专业标准

> ⚠️ **来源说明：** 以下内容基于 CFA Institute 公开的 Code of Ethics and Standards of Professional Conduct 框架整理。具体内容以主人提供的教材为准。

## Code of Ethics（道德准则）

CFA 会员及考生应：

1. Act with integrity, competence, diligence, respect, and in an ethical manner with the public, clients, prospective clients, employers, employees, colleagues in the investment profession, and other participants in the global capital markets.
2. Place the integrity of the investment profession and the interests of clients above their own personal interests.
3. Use reasonable care and exercise independent professional judgment.
4. Practice and encourage others to practice in a professional and ethical manner.
5. Promote the integrity of, and uphold the rules governing, capital markets.
6. Maintain and improve their professional competence.

## Standards of Professional Conduct（七大行为准则）

### I. Professionalism（专业性）
- **A.** Knowledge of the Law — 了解并遵守适用法律法规
- **B.** Independence and Objectivity — 保持独立性和客观性
- **C.** Misrepresentation — 不得虚假陈述
- **D.** Misconduct — 不得有不当行为

### II. Integrity of Capital Markets（资本市场诚信）
- **A.** Material Nonpublic Information — 不得利用重大非公开信息
- **B.** Market Manipulation — 不得操纵市场

### III. Duties to Clients（对客户的义务）
- **A.** Loyalty, Prudence, and Care — 忠诚、审慎、尽责
- **B.** Fair Dealing — 公平对待
- **C.** Suitability — 适当性
- **D.** Performance Presentation — 业绩呈报
- **E.** Preservation of Confidentiality — 保密

### IV. Duties to Employers（对雇主的义务）
- **A.** Loyalty — 忠诚义务
- **B.** Additional Compensation Arrangements — 额外报酬安排
- **C.** Responsibilities of Supervisors — 监督责任

### V. Investment Analysis, Recommendations, and Actions（投资分析与建议）
- **A.** Diligence and Reasonable Basis — 勤勉与合理基础
- **B.** Communication with Clients and Prospective Clients — 与客户沟通
- **C.** Record Keeping — 记录保存

### VI. Conflicts of Interest（利益冲突）
- **A.** Disclosure of Conflicts — 披露利益冲突
- **B.** Priority of Transactions — 交易优先顺序
- **C.** Referral Fees — 推荐费

### VII. Responsibilities as a CFA Institute Member（CFA会员责任）
- **A.** Conduct as Participants in CFA Institute Programs
- **B.** Reference to CFA Institute, the CFA Designation, and the CFA Program

## GIPS（全球投资业绩标准）

- Global Investment Performance Standards
- 旨在提供公平、可比较的投资业绩报告
- 基于**全公司**（firm-wide）而非单一产品
- 关键要求：实际收益率、费用后业绩、基准对比
