# 🗣️ 健康 Health

## 场景简介
> 描述身体不适、看医生、康复等健康相关场景的英语表达。无论是感冒发烧还是去看牙医，这些词汇都能帮你准确描述症状。

## 核心词汇

### 身体不适

#### 1. Aches and pains
- **英文**：Aches and pains
- **中文**：各种疼痛（持续的小疼痛）
- **例句**：I've been having aches and pains all week.（我这周一直各种疼痛。）

#### 2. To be a bit off colour
- **英文**：To be a bit off colour
- **中文**：有点不舒服（口语）
- **例句**：You look a bit off colour — are you OK?（你看起来有点不舒服——还好吗？）

#### 3. To be under the weather
- **英文**：To be under the weather
- **中文**：身体不舒服（口语）
- **例句**：I'm feeling a bit under the weather today.（我今天感觉有点不舒服。）

#### 4. To feel poorly
- **英文**：To feel poorly
- **中文**：感觉不舒服
- **例句**：The children are feeling poorly.（孩子们感觉不舒服。）

#### 5. To be at death's door
- **英文**：To be at death's door
- **中文**：病得很重（口语，夸张说法）
- **例句**：He had the flu and acted like he was at death's door.（他得了流感，表现得像病入膏肓一样。）

### 感冒症状

#### 6. To catch a cold
- **英文**：To catch a cold
- **中文**：感冒了
- **例句**：I caught a cold in the rain.（我在雨中感冒了。）

#### 7. A blocked nose
- **英文**：A blocked nose
- **中文**：鼻塞
- **例句**：I have a blocked nose and can't breathe properly.（我鼻塞，呼吸不畅。）

#### 8. A runny nose
- **英文**：A runny nose
- **中文**：流鼻涕
- **例句**：I've had a runny nose all day.（我一整天都在流鼻涕。）

#### 9. A sore throat
- **英文**：A sore throat
- **中文**：喉咙痛
- **例句**：I have a sore throat and a cough.（我喉咙痛还咳嗽。）

#### 10. A chesty cough
- **英文**：A chesty cough
- **中文**：有痰的咳嗽
- **例句**：She's got a chesty cough that won't go away.（她的有痰咳嗽一直不好。）

#### 11. A heavy cold
- **英文**：A heavy cold
- **中文**：重感冒
- **例句**：He's in bed with a heavy cold.（他得了重感冒卧床不起。）

#### 12. Cuts and bruises
- **英文**：Cuts and bruises
- **中文**：割伤和淤伤
- **例句**：He came home with cuts and bruises after the match.（比赛后他带着割伤和淤伤回家了。）

### 康复

#### 13. To be on the mend
- **英文**：To be on the mend
- **中文**：正在康复
- **例句**：I had the flu last week, but I'm on the mend now.（我上周得了流感，但现在正在康复。）

#### 14. To be over the worst
- **英文**：To be over the worst
- **中文**：度过了最严重的阶段
- **例句**：The doctor says I'm over the worst.（医生说我度过了最严重的阶段。）

#### 15. To make a speedy recovery
- **英文**：To make a speedy recovery
- **中文**：快速康复
- **例句**：I hope you make a speedy recovery.（希望你快速康复。）

### 看医生

#### 16. A check-up
- **英文**：A check-up
- **中文**：体检/检查
- **例句**：I go for a check-up every year.（我每年做一次体检。）

#### 17. To make an appointment
- **英文**：To make an appointment
- **中文**：预约
- **例句**：I need to make an appointment with the dentist.（我需要预约牙医。）

#### 18. GP
- **英文**：GP（General Practitioner）
- **中文**：全科医生/家庭医生
- **例句**：You should see your GP about that cough.（你应该去看全科医生治疗咳嗽。）

#### 19. To phone in sick
- **英文**：To phone in sick
- **中文**：打电话请病假
- **例句**：I had to phone in sick this morning.（我今天早上不得不打电话请病假。）

#### 20. To go private
- **英文**：To go private
- **中文**：选择私立医疗（自费）
- **例句**：She decided to go private to avoid the long waiting list.（她决定选择私立医疗以避免长长的等候名单。）

### 牙科

#### 21. To have a filling
- **英文**：To have a filling
- **中文**：补牙
- **例句**：I need to have a filling next week.（我下周需要去补牙。）

#### 22. To have a tooth out
- **英文**：To have a tooth out
- **中文**：拔牙
- **例句**：The dentist said I need to have a tooth out.（牙医说我需要拔一颗牙。）

### 其他

#### 23. As fit as a fiddle
- **英文**：As fit as a fiddle
- **中文**：非常健康
- **例句**：My grandfather is 80 and as fit as a fiddle.（我爷爷80岁了，身体非常健康。）

#### 24. To pull a muscle
- **英文**：To pull a muscle
- **中文**：拉伤肌肉
- **例句**：I pulled a muscle in my back at the gym.（我在健身房拉伤了背部肌肉。）

#### 25. Prescription charges
- **英文**：Prescription charges
- **中文**：处方药费用
- **例句**：Prescription charges have gone up this year.（今年处方药费用涨了。）

## 常用句型
- I'm feeling a bit under the weather.（我感觉有点不舒服。）
- I need to make an appointment with the doctor.（我需要预约医生。）
- I've been having aches and pains all week.（我这周一直各种疼痛。）
- I hope you make a speedy recovery.（希望你快速康复。）
- I had to phone in sick today.（我今天不得不请病假。）

## 📝 学习建议
- 学会用 "I have..." 和 "I feel..." 描述症状
- 记住常用习语：under the weather、off colour、fit as a fiddle
- 模拟看医生的对话场景，练习实际运用
